How this works

Enter your current station and up to 5 stations you would like to move to. The system looks for a circle of people whose wishes fit together, up to {{ N }} people. Each person moves to the station of the next person in the circle.

Example: a 6-person chain

PersonNow atWantsGets
ADelhiPunePune (B's station)
BPuneChennaiChennai (C's station)
CChennaiMumbaiMumbai (D's station)
DMumbaiKolkataKolkata (E's station)
EKolkataJaipurJaipur (F's station)
FJaipurDelhiDelhi (A's station)

A → B → C → D → E → F → A. No two of them could swap directly, but together all six get what they asked for.

The rules

• Neighbouring people in the circle must be within ±{{ K }} designation level(s) of each other.
• Everyone in the circle sees the other members and their contact details, then each person accepts or rejects.
• If anyone rejects, that circle is cancelled and everyone else goes back to the pool for new matches.
• If you don't need a transfer, choose "Currently no transfer needed" as Choice 1. You can still log in and browse.
• Shorter circles are tried first. Earlier registrations get priority.
• If a match fails only because of a designation gap, you may see an "Almost match" note. Names are never shown.
